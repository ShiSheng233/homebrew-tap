## About

Transcriber turns audio and video into subtitles on your Mac. Recognition runs locally with Whisper
models on the GPU and the Neural Engine; an optional last step sends the subtitles to a language model
of your choice to polish or translate them.

- Queue several files.
- Edit the result in the built-in subtitle editor and re-transcribe a range.
- Export to the usual subtitle formats.

## Models

Models are not bundled with the app. Put them in the models folder set in Settings, by default
`~/Library/Application Support/Transcriber/Models`.

<Aside title="Language model API keys">
  Keys for the language model step are stored in your login keychain, so `brew uninstall --zap` leaves
  them in place.
</Aside>
